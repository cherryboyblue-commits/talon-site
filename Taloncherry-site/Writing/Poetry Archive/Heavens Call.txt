A long time,
Maybe one hundred years
From now
Ill find a place to breathe
On a cold night
After twilight
The crimson sky
Will reign down
And torch the ocean floor
 
In song that sings and rings across the endless beaches
Past the meadows where the lions have a place to lay
Ill find that place on earth where hell cant touch and heaven reaches
Space and time and maybe float away
 
Follow Alice and the Rabbit through the castle breaches
Forge a key that opens every other corner door
Climb the wishing well and toss a thousand silver pieces
Listen as the wishes hit the floor
 
Maybe someday peter pan will take my daughter flying
To never land to watch the shadows as they sing and play
Away from bullets flying people killing children crying
The star where no one ever fades away
 
I want to see the angels crawl out from the white sand dollars
Ring a bell until they grow their wings and hit the sky
Capture every moment just before they break earths collar
Believe in God and never wonder why 
 
On this day so far away you know ill be in heaven
Never again to let the sneaky people bring me down
Walking with angels while the clocks are ringing one eleven
And seven forty sevens make no sound
