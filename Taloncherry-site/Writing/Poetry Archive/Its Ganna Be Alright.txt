It's Ganna Be Alright

July 29, 2009. © Talon Truitt Cherry, All rights reserved

When life speeds by faster than your feet can carry.
When burdens grow greater, and you grow weary.
When time is short, and feelings vary.

Slow it down my brother.
It's ganna be alright.
Your ganna be alright.

When skies are grey almost every day.
When your walls of control crumble like clay.
No will to live, No will to stay.

Hold on my brother.
It's ganna be alright.
Your ganna be alright.

When god always seams just out of reach.
Your savior has left town, and your knees grow week.
When you pray so often and so loud that you can barely speak!

Quiet down my brother.
It's ganna be alright.
Your ganna be alright.

Not even the loudest of screams are heard by god.
Pray from your heart and god will nod.
Its your heart that speaks when your voice cannot.

Believe in that my brother,
and it'll be alright.
You'll be alright
